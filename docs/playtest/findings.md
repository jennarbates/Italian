## Running a session

- Before: open the staging URL on your own phone, play one round, and check it still works.
- Give the tester [the note](tester-note.md). Don't explain the game beyond it.
- Time each round from tapping **Play** to the round end screen.
- Write down what they say and where they hesitate. Help only when they've been stuck for a minute, and log that you did.
- After the third round, ask the three questions at the bottom.
