# Chi è? playtest, Thursday, October 22

Thanks for helping. Chi è? is a Guess Who style game for learning Italian at A1. You and the computer each have a secret character. Take turns asking yes/no questions in Italian until one of you guesses the other's character.

## Before you start

- Use your own phone and the browser you usually use.
- Open **<!-- TODO: stable staging URL -->**
- You don't need an account. Play as a guest and skip any "Sign in" buttons. Your rounds are saved on your phone.
- This is a test version. Nothing you do here affects anyone else.

## What to do

Play **3 rounds**. Take as long as you need.

1. Pick **Level 1** (ready-made questions with English hints) for your first round.
2. If Level 1 feels easy, try **Level 2** (build each question from word tiles) for the next two.
3. Press and hold a card to see the character up close.
4. Think aloud if you can: say what you're trying to do, and anything that surprises you.

## What we want to hear about

- Any Italian you didn't understand, or that seemed wrong
- Any button, message or step that confused you
- Whether you won or lost each round, and how long it felt
- Anything you liked

There are no wrong answers. If you get stuck, that's the most useful thing we can learn, so say so before asking for help.
